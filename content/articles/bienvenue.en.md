Welcome to Sorcières Mortelles.

These woods are a collaborative space for sharing around art. Here you will find interviews with artists and articles on all kinds of artistic subjects. The site aims to share human art, and the way humans experience it.

Anyone can suggest topics for articles, or even articles already written, as well as artists to interview (yourself included!). To do so, simply send us ~~an e-mail~~ a letter through the [Contact](/contact/) page.

We hope to see you again in these woods.

> All of Us Witches, All of Us Artists.
> So let us share our magic and our art.
